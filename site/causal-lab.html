WORKSPACE 05 · NEXT OBSERVATION
What measurement would reduce uncertainty most?
INFORMATION GAIN


Rank candidate follow-up times by expected reduction in model uncertainty. Forecasts must come from a physical model or supplied measurement analysis. Visibility is accepted only when independently calculated.


Each candidate forecast needs candidate_observation_id, time, measurement_option, predictions for every competing fitted model, and forecast_provenance with fit_id and input_observation_ids for each model. Recommendation also requires a structured feasibility record: verified status, feasible boolean, HTTPS source_uri, checked_utc, calculator, query_id, evidence_sha256 and query_parameters. Do not use invented visibility windows.
Alternative priors are compared with the candidate forecasts. A ranked forecast is not a visibility calculation.
RANKED EXPERIMENTS
The highest expected information gain will be ranked first.

THE SCIENTIFIC STANDARD
Unknown is not failure.
It is a result the evidence has earned.
CHRONOS will call a feature complete only after it survives calibration checks, negative controls, independent field-held-out testing and reproducible analysis on real archive data.
