LAB 01

Time-resolved spectral comparison

102-channel compatible

Compare two spectra on their native overlapping wavelength samples. The tool reports flux differences without significance unless both spectra contain valid uncertainty arrays.

● Reference● Comparison
Paste two measured spectra or load the clearly labelled synthetic example.

All-Sky Cube NHITS is a contributor count, not variance. Channels marked blank remain missing. For individual spectral-image science wavelengths, use the separate CWAVE/CBAND calibration map.

LAB 02

Spectral feature measurement

Equivalent width

Measure a user-specified absorption/emission feature against two continuum sidebands. This routine measures a feature; it does not identify a molecule.

Load or paste a spectrum in Lab 01 first.
LAB 03

Instrument forensics

Transparent rules, not AI

Enter measured diagnostics from the relevant exposure, detector and calibration version. These checks generate review leads, not an automatic artefact verdict.

Run checks on measurements from a real candidate.

Template overlap is meaningful only when the product is from the correct detector, processing version and pixel grid. Review official flag definitions.

LAB 04

Next-observation optimizer

Expected information gain

Rank feasible follow-up opportunities by expected ability to distinguish competing hypotheses. Supply forecasts from physical models and a real visibility calculation.

Illustrative forecasts will not be represented as real ephemerides.
LAB 05

Injection and recovery accounting

Independent field validation

Calculate one-to-one recovery precision, completeness and false candidates per field. For a benchmark, split by independent sky field and publish recovery by brightness, detector, wavelength, coverage and crowding.

Example numbers are illustrative. Replace with measured blind-test results.
LAB 06

Catalogue cross-match

Spherical geometry

Find the nearest positional catalogue entry inside an angular radius. A positional match is a lead, not proof of a physical counterpart. Coordinates are decimal degrees.

LAB 07

Scientific evidence passport

SHA-256 integrity seal

Record source data, calibration, measurements, methods and limitations. This seal detects later content changes; it is not a digital signature, identity proof or independent scientific validation.
