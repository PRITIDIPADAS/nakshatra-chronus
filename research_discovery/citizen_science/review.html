CHRONOS: human review pilot

Offline annotation prototype. It does not upload data. Research deployment requires consent, institutional review where applicable, privacy review, expert-labelled controls and a public project owner.

Consent

Participation is voluntary. Do not enter your name, email, location or other identifying information. Your classifications may be exported for research only after the study owner supplies an approved consent and data-use statement.
