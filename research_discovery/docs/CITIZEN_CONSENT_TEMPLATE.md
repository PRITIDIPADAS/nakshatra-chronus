# CHRONOS volunteer research pilot: participant information and consent template

**Draft for institutional review only. Do not use for recruitment until an appropriate ethics committee/institution has approved the study and all placeholders are completed.**

## Study title

Human review of time-separated astronomical image cutouts for candidate triage

## Invitation

You are invited to take part in a research pilot evaluating whether people can consistently classify simple pairs of astronomical image cutouts. The task concerns image interpretation only. It does not ask participants to identify Planet Nine or make scientific discovery claims.

## What you will do

You will view a randomized set of pairs of image cutouts. For each pair, you will select one label: moving source, stationary source, image artifact, or uncertain. You may also provide a confidence rating. The pilot is expected to take approximately [approved duration]. You may skip a case or stop at any time.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may stop without giving a reason. The approved study plan must specify whether and how already-submitted anonymous responses can be withdrawn after submission; this must be explained here before launch: [withdrawal procedure and deadline].

## Risks and benefits

The expected risk is low and mainly involves time and possible visual fatigue. There is no guaranteed direct benefit. The results may help researchers evaluate human review as a way to prioritize astronomical image candidates.

## Data collected

The study should collect only the task labels, confidence ratings, task IDs and the minimum technical metadata required for analysis. Do not collect names, personal emails, birth dates, precise location or unrelated personal details unless separately approved and necessary.
